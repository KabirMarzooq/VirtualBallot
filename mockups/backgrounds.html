Background System — proposal · revision 2

Three treatments for the screens that are currently flat white or flat gray-50.

For approval — nothing here is implemented yet. Revision 2 reworks the expanding rings into a much more present Signal Ring system, and adds two new interactive layers (grid reveal, drifting motes). Use the control panel below to dial the rings in — whatever numbers you settle on are what I'll build.
Ring lab
Signal Rings — before and after

The approved auth background emits 3 rings on a 4s loop, staggered 1.3s apart, in blue-200 at 50% opacity — so in practice you usually see one faint ring at a time. The reworked system emits more rings, holds them longer, starts them brighter, and gives them a much wider travel, so four or five are always mid-flight at once. Left is what's live today; right is the proposal.

BG-1
Aurora Field + Signal Rings — centred card pages

Drifting blue blobs over white, with the reworked rings emitting from behind the card and the pointer adding parallax — blobs shift a few pixels against the cursor, so the field has depth without anything visibly chasing you. Clicking fires an extra ring from the pointer, which reads naturally on a voting platform as a signal being sent.

BG-2
Spotlight Grid + Grid Reveal — app shells

Still the quietest of the three — these screens carry dense tables and stat grids, and the background must never compete with them. But it now does something worth noticing: the dots within ~190px of the pointer brighten from blue-200 to blue-500, so the grid feels lit rather than printed. No rings here, and nothing moves on its own — the only motion is the one the operator is already causing.

BG-3
Ribbon Wash + Drifting Motes — long content pages

These pages scroll a long way, so pointer-linked motion would become noise. Instead the depth comes from two fixed corner washes and a diagonal seam, now joined by a slow field of motes rising through the page — enough life to stop it reading as flat, slow enough to ignore while reading.

Each organisation is responsible for the accuracy of its own voter roster, for the conduct of its election committee, and for any decision arising from a tied result.

Ballots are written once and never updated. Each vote is appended to a hash chain whose entries include the previous entry's hash, so altering any past vote breaks every entry recorded after it.

Implementation rules
How these get built
RuleDetail
one componentExtend AuthBackground.jsx into PageBackground.jsx with a variant prop (aurora / spotlight / ribbon / auth / auth-dark). Ring config lives in CSS vars so it can be tuned in one place afterwards.
the auth pagesThey currently use the 3-ring version. Once you pick values I'd apply the same rings there too, so the whole product reads as one system — say if you'd rather leave auth alone.
never intercepts inputAll layers position:fixed; inset:0; pointer-events:none; z-index:0; content sits at z-index:1. The click ripple listens on the page, not the layer.
contrast unchangedCards stay solid --white. Body text never sits directly on a wash or a ring, so every existing contrast ratio is preserved.
reduced motionprefers-reduced-motion freezes rings at 18% opacity, stops blobs and motes, and drops the spotlight and grid reveal entirely.
performanceCompositor-only (transform/opacity), will-change on the animated layers. Ring count is capped — 10 elements is well inside budget.
printAll decorative layers hidden in @media print — receipts and reports print clean.
